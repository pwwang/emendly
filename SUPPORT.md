# Supporting Emendly

Emendly is MIT licensed and free. Every feature ships in this repository — there is
no paid tier, no licence key and no trial.

If it saves you time, you can support the work in two ways.

## GitHub Sponsors

[github.com/sponsors/pwwang](https://github.com/sponsors/pwwang)

Monthly or one-time. Sponsors get their name listed in `SPONSORS.md` if they want it.

## What donations pay for

Donations go to the costs that keep a desktop utility shipping, in this order:

| Cost | Amount | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Code-signing certificate | $10/month | An unsigned installer shows a SmartScreen warning that most people abandon |
| Apple Developer Program, once macOS ships | $99/year | Required for a notarised macOS build |
| Domain `emendly.dev` | ~$12/year | The download page |
| Nothing else | — | No servers, no hosted inference, no staff |

There is no hosted inference to fund. Emendly sends your text straight to the provider
you configure, with your own key, so inference is billed to you by that provider.

## What donations do not buy

- Priority support is not for sale. Issues are handled in the open, in order.
- Donations do not influence the roadmap. Features are chosen by what breaks and what
  users report, not by who paid.

## Transparency

Costs and any sponsorship income are listed in `SPONSORS.md`. If donations stop
covering signing, the certificate lapses and the app ships unsigned again — I would
rather say that plainly than pretend a funding model exists that does not.
